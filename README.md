# My Favorite Movie: Maleficent

## About the Movie
My favorite movie is **Maleficent**.

Maleficent is a fantasy movie about a powerful fairy who protects her magical kingdom. The story shows her journey, love, betrayal, and forgiveness.

## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because:
- Maleficent is powerful and brave.
- The story is interesting and magical.
- It teaches lessons about love and forgiveness.
- The movie has beautiful visual effects.
